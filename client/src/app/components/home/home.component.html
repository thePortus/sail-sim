Bay of Pirates

A living archipelago of trade, war & piracy — multiplayer, open early access

Sail a wind-bound ship across an archipelago held by four warring colonial powers. Trade goods on a market that breathes with supply and demand, and make your name — as an honest merchant captain or a pirate preying on the sea-lanes.

  • ⚓ True sailing — wind, points of sail, sheet trim & tacking
  • ⚖ Living economy — buy low, sell high; prices react to your trades
  • ⚔ Factions & diplomacy — four nations shift between war, peace & alliance
  • 🏴‍☠️ Piracy & reputation — raid a nation's shipping to earn its enemies' favour
  • 💥 Ship combat — broadsides, dismasting, crew & floating salvage
  • 👥 Crew & squadrons — recruit hands and sail together, up to four
